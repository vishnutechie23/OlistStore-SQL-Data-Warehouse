A. Dimension tables
1. gold.dim_customers
Source: silver.olist_customers

Column	Purpose

B. Fact tables
Fact tables project ka main analytical part hain.
7. gold.fact_orders
Grain: One row per order
Source: Silver Orders + Customers.
Column	Purpose
order_id	Order identifier
customer_key	Customer dimension reference
order_status_key	Order status reference
purchase_date_key	Purchase date
approved_date_key	Approval date
carrier_date_key	Carrier handover date
delivered_date_key	Actual delivery date
estimated_delivery_date_key	Estimated delivery date
order_count	Always 1; order counting
approval_days	Purchase to approval duration
delivery_days	Purchase to delivery duration
estimated_delivery_days	Purchase to estimated delivery duration
delay_days	Actual delivery minus estimated date
is_delivered	Delivered flag
is_late_delivery	Late delivery flag

Business use:
- Total orders.
- Delivered vs canceled orders.
- Average delivery time.
- Late delivery percentage.
- Monthly order trends.
Important: Missing delivery dates ko automatically late nahi maanenge. Late delivery sirf valid delivered orders ke basis par calculate karenge.
8. gold.fact_order_items
Grain: One row per order item line
Source: Silver Order Items + Orders + Customers + Products + Sellers.
Column	Purpose
order_id	Order identifier
order_item_id	Item sequence in order
customer_key	Customer reference
product_key	Product reference
seller_key	Seller reference
purchase_date_key	Purchase date
shipping_limit_date_key	Shipping deadline
item_count	Always 1
price	Product price
freight_value	Freight cost
item_total_value	Price + freight

Business use:
- Total product sales.
- Total freight charges.
- Best-selling products.
- Category-wise revenue.
- Seller-wise sales.
- Average order value (with distinct order counting).
Very important: Is fact table ka grain item-level hai. Ek order mein multiple products ho sakte hain.
9. gold.fact_payments
Grain: One row per payment transaction
Source: Silver Order Payments + Orders + Customers.
Column	Purpose
order_id	Order identifier
payment_sequential	Payment sequence
customer_key	Customer reference
purchase_date_key	Related purchase date
payment_type_key	Payment method reference
payment_installments	Number of installments
payment_value	Payment amount
payment_count	Always 1

Business use:
- Total payment value.
- Payment method distribution.
- Installment analysis.
- Average payment amount.
Important: Payment aur order items ko directly join karke sales/payment SUM nahi karenge. Dono ka grain different hai, isse duplicate aggregation ho sakti hai.
10. gold.fact_reviews
Grain: One row per review record
Source: Silver Reviews + Orders + Customers.
Column	Purpose
review_key	Review identifier from Silver
review_id	Original review ID
order_id	Related order
customer_key	Customer reference
review_creation_date_key	Review creation date
review_answer_date_key	Review answer date
review_score	Rating from 1 to 5
review_count	Always 1

Business use:
- Average review score.
- 1-star vs 5-star reviews.
- Monthly customer satisfaction.
- Review trends by category/customer.
Review comments ko fact table mein include karne ki zarurat nahi; detailed text analysis future enhancement ho sakta hai.

5. Olist dataset se kaunse business problems solve kar sakte hain?
Project ko sirf SQL practice nahi banana. Humein business questions ke answers dene hain.
Business problem	Analysis
Sales performance	Monthly, yearly and category-wise sales
Delivery delays	Late orders and average delivery time
Order cancellations	Canceled and unavailable order trends
Customer retention	Repeat vs one-time customers
Product performance	Top/bottom selling categories
Seller performance	Sales and delivery performance by seller
Payment preferences	Credit card, boleto, voucher etc.
Customer satisfaction	Review score and low-rating trends
Freight analysis	Freight contribution by category and location
Geographic performance	State/city-wise sales and orders

Important dataset limitations
Olist dataset se hum ye directly calculate nahi kar sakte:
- Actual profit (product cost/margin available nahi).
- Inventory stock or stockout.
- Marketing campaign ROI.
- Customer demographics.
- Actual return/refund rate (complete returns data nahi).
Hum revenue-like sales measures calculate kar sakte hain, but sales revenue ko profit nahi bolenge.

Dataset 2016–2018 ka historical data hai. Isliye dashboard ko historical business analysis ke roop mein present karenge.
